# Individual reflection guide
Answer in your own words and use real examples from your own commits.

1. Biggest technical challenge: describe the issue, how you investigated it, what you changed and how you tested the fix.
2. Roles held: list each role and one skill learned in each.
3. Client change request: describe the actual change card your team received, how the backlog changed and regression tests performed.
4. Security: explain password hashing, validation, permissions, ORM protection, XSS/CSRF controls and secrets management.
5. Teamwork: describe a real disagreement or missed deadline and how it was handled professionally.
6. Skills to develop: identify specific gaps and a practical learning plan.
